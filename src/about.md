Hi! I'm Amber ([she/her][pronouns]), I'm {{ meta.data.authorAge }}, and this is
my website.

I'm an _everything hobbyist_, weaving in and out of many different interests,
including but not limited to:

- cars (sportscar racing, endurance racing, modified cars)

- computers, and their various uses:
  - linux (I use [Arch][arch] btw)
  - programming, as a hobby (including JavaScript, Ruby, Rust, Go, and others)
  - computer-aided-design (CAD)
  - electronic engineering (EE)
  - mechanical keyboards, both vintage and modern customs
  - video games, mostly of the late 1990s/early 2000s but also including modern
    motorsport and vehicle simulations
    - I briefly had a fixation with flight simulators

- music
  - I have a growing collection of Japanese jazz-fusion
  - I own more MiniDiscs than you, probably
  - I have several hundred gigabytes of Vaporwave, from _Chuck Person_ to
    _desert sand_ and everyone in between
  - I have four cassette decks

- film photography, 35mm and '645' medium format

If it's something I can customise or modify I've gone down the rabbit hole,
complete with the hours-long Wikipedia deep dive and trawling forum threads.
This kind of deep research and involvement with hobby subculture is extremely
rewarding and I hope to continue finding things to obsess over.
